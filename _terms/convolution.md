A convolution is an operation where one function is moved along another function. 

f usually stays in place while g moves across the x-axis. At each t, integral of the sum of the two functions is calculated from -∞ to +∞.

![Convolution](/assets/images/convolution.svg)

The animations [here](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Convolution.html) are very useful to visualize this. It also shows that the convolution of a box with another box has a triangle shape (for a box convolved with itself: perfect triangle).
